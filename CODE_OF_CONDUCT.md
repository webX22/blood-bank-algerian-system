# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Rifaq project to be respectful, welcoming, and focused on improving a technical coordination foundation. Contributors, maintainers, and community members should be able to collaborate without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, constructive, and respectful of differing experience and perspectives.
- Give actionable feedback about code and ideas; criticize the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy. Never publish real patient, donor, health, account, or facility-confidential information.
- Describe medical, legal, geographic, security, and accessibility limitations accurately. Do not present this project as authorized for clinical use.
- Follow maintainers' directions when they are acting to protect the community or project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted contact, and publishing private or sensitive data are not acceptable. Deliberately misrepresenting this software as clinically approved, or using the project to encourage unsafe real-world medical decisions, is also outside the purpose of this community.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing or directly interacting with the project community.

Project maintainers will review reports, may remove content, restrict participation, or close contributions, and will apply measures proportionately to the circumstances. Maintainers may not be able to investigate or resolve matters outside project-controlled spaces.

To report conduct, contact the maintainer privately through the [project owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/webX22). Do not post identifying or sensitive details in a public issue. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Acknowledgment

This is a project-specific policy informed by the goals of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). It does not claim clinical, legal, regulatory, or institutional endorsement.
